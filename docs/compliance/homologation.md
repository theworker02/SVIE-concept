# Homologation Path (Design Intent)

**Release:** 1.3.0 · **SoT:** [`specs/homologation_path.yaml`](../../specs/homologation_path.yaml)

US (EPA/CARB), EU (WLTP/RDE), and JP (MLIT) paths are documented as **post-TRL-4** programs. Fuels: gasoline SFV and H₂ DI.

**Today’s blockers:** no dyno certificates, no production BOM quotes, no OEM vehicle integration.

**Hardware sequence:** G1 spray → G2 single-cylinder → G3 multi-cylinder mule.

This pack is acquisition diligence for *path clarity*, not a type-approval certificate.
